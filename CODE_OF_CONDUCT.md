# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being considerate and respectful.
- Offering and receiving constructive feedback graciously.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
behavior they consider unacceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project or its community in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers using the contact
details listed in the repository. Reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially to the extent practical.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the impact and context of each incident. Responses
may include a warning, temporary restriction from project spaces, or permanent
exclusion from the community.
